# Mira

## One-line summary

**Mandatory.** A kitchen-side observer in the keep whose partial view of the grain thefts drives a chain of wrong then corrected conclusions — the pressure that keeps the theft subplot alive before the truth surfaces.

## Narrative function

**Mandatory.**

- Primary role: the character through whom the reader tracks who is *believed* to be the thief, scene by scene, ahead of who actually is.
- Pressure she brings: she acts on what she believes, so a wrong belief has consequences before it is corrected.
- What breaks without her: the theft subplot loses its point-of-view onto suspicion, and the mid-book reveal has no one to have been wrong first.

## Core identity

**Mandatory.**

- Age: 20s.
- Species / type: human.
- Occupation / social role: under-cook and stores-keeper's assistant in the baron's keep.
- Home / base: the keep's kitchens and stores.
- Affiliations: the keep's household staff.
- Status: below-stairs; trusted with the tally-book but not with the household's confidences.

## Essence

**Mandatory.**

- **Central desire.** To be believed by people who outrank her.
- **Central fear.** Being blamed for a loss she only reported.
- **Misbelief.** That the loudest, most confident account of events is the true one.
- **Wound / formative damage.** TBD.
- **Greatest strength.** She notices discrepancies in the tallies no one else reads closely.
- **Greatest flaw.** She commits to a conclusion the moment someone she trusts states it plainly.
- **Default coping strategy.** Gather more of the same evidence rather than test the conclusion.
- **Moral line she will not cross.** Falsifying the tally-book herself.
- **Moral line she might cross under pressure.** Accusing the wrong person out loud to clear her own name.

## Voice and presence

**Mandatory.**

- **Surface personality.** Watchful, precise, deferential.
- **Private personality.** Stubborn once convinced.
- **Social mask.** The reliable underling who only reports what she can count.
- **Speech patterns.** Short, concrete sentences; leans on numbers and what she saw.
- **Physical presence.** Small, quick, tends to the edges of a room.
- **Tells, habits, mannerisms.** Counts on her fingers when she is uncertain.

## Continuity constraints

**Mandatory.**

- **Immutable facts.** Mira keeps the stores tally-book in book 1.
- **Forbidden contradictions.** She must not be written as having known the captain was the thief before book1/chapter07/scene02 (see knowledge/book-1.md).
- **Timing constraints.** She must not learn the captain acts on the baron's orders until book1/chapter09/scene04 (the active ## Must not know yet constraint in knowledge/book-1.md).
