# Rin

## One-line summary

**Mandatory.** The *Meridian*'s bosun, the crew member who first finds the water casks breached and fixes — wrongly — on the cook as the culprit, keeping the shipboard suspicion pointed the wrong way while the truth stays hidden.

## Narrative function

**Mandatory.**

- Primary role: the character through whom the reader tracks who is *believed* to have breached the casks, ahead of who objectively did.
- Pressure she brings: as bosun she can act on her belief — she can watch, accuse, and set the crew against the man she suspects.
- What breaks without her: the sabotage subplot loses its point-of-view onto suspicion, and the objective answer has no one to have been wrong first.

## Core identity

**Mandatory.**

- Age: 30s.
- Species / type: human.
- Occupation / social role: bosun of the *Meridian*; keeps the deck crew and the ship's stores in order.
- Home / base: the *Meridian*'s deck and forward hold.
- Affiliations: the ship's company under Captain Voss, then Halden.
- Status: a warrant officer — trusted with the stores, not with the officers' confidences.

## Essence

**Mandatory.**

- **Central desire.** To keep the ship's company whole and provisioned across the crossing.
- **Central fear.** That sabotage from within will strand them without water.
- **Misbelief.** That the man she already mistrusts is the man at fault.
- **Wound / formative damage.** TBD.
- **Greatest strength.** She knows the hold and the stores better than anyone aboard.
- **Greatest flaw.** She lets an old grudge decide where she looks first.
- **Moral line she will not cross.** Falsifying the stores tally herself.
- **Moral line she might cross under pressure.** Accusing the wrong man to the crew before she is sure.

## Voice and presence

**Mandatory.**

- **Surface personality.** Brisk, watchful, exacting about the stores.
- **Private personality.** Slow to forgive a quarrel.
- **Social mask.** The steady bosun who only reports what she can count.
- **Speech patterns.** Short, concrete, weighted toward what she saw in the hold.
- **Physical presence.** Broad-handed, sure-footed on a pitching deck.
- **Tells, habits, mannerisms.** Taps the cask staves as she counts them.

## Continuity constraints

**Mandatory.**

- **Immutable facts.** Rin is the *Meridian*'s bosun in book 1 and first finds the breached casks (co-03 in continuity/book-1.md).
- **Forbidden contradictions.** She must not be written as knowing the quartermaster breached the casks before the crew's exposure of his ledger (see knowledge/book-1.md).
- **Timing constraints.** She must not learn who actually breached the casks until book1/chapter04/scene02 (the active ## Must not know yet constraint in knowledge/book-1.md).
