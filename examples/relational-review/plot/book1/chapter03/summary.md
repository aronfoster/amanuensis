# Chapter 3 summary

Two days after the storm, Wick finds the *Cormorant*'s manifest four casks short and takes the discrepancy to Captain Fenn. The exchange tests the voyage's day count, the crew's memory of recent losses, the Saltford account, Wick's limited knowledge, and the ship's watch roster without yet revealing who altered the manifest.
