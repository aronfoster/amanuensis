# Beat

Dessa comes back down off the ridge at dusk, dust-pale and grinning, and reports Drybone's lights sitting plain on the horizon at last. Let her read of the wind-scour on the way down carry a trace of trained habit — she came up through a salt-diviner's apprenticeship before this crew, and it shows in how she watches weather sign, though the prose need not spell out the biography. Torrin passes the word down the line to make ready.
