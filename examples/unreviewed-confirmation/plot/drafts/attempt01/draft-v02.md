Senna watched Iyo Brakk kneel over the lashed crate at the edge of Last Well's stone
trough, chalk in hand. The hedge-mage worked the ward-sigil in slow blue strokes — blue
ochre, not the caravan's usual white chalk, since the salt wind ate white chalk raw
within a day. "Three days," Iyo said, more to the ward than to anyone listening, "then
it wants doing again." The verglass under the lashings sat dark and solid, sweating
nothing.

<!-- block 002 -->

By the second recast, Senna had stopped counting the days on her fingers and started
counting them by the wear on Iyo's chalk hand. "Every two days, same as always," Iyo
said, already scraping the old sigil clear to redraw it. The verglass held glass-hard
under her palm, no give at all.

<!-- block 003 -->

Senna reckoned it out again that night: nine days out of Last Well, and Drybone Market
still two ridges off. Her boots had gone white to the ankle with crusted salt, and her
water skin rode lighter than she liked.
